About

Who I am

I'm a Computer Engineering major (Business minor) at UC Davis, currently interning at Solidigm on NAND design automation — writing Python and scripting tooling that supports production physical design flows. My background spans FPGA/Verilog and RTL fundamentals, photonic simulation research, and Python/ML tooling (NumPy, pandas, PyTorch), and I'm focused on applying machine learning and optimization to make chip design and EDA flows faster and more efficient.

Research

Photonic simulation, nanofabrication & design optimization

Research Intern — Integrated Nano Systems and Nano Devices Lab · California-Pacific-Northwest AI Hardware Hub · Apr 2025 – Mar 2026

I designed diatom-inspired photonic structures to optimize light concentration and photon trapping for on-chip applications, running simulation-driven design-space exploration and training in cleanroom fabrication to help bring those designs from simulation to physical devices.

  • Photonic Simulation

    Modeled diatom-inspired 3D geometries in Blender and CAD to optimize light concentration and photon trapping.

  • Grating Optimization

    Ran FDTD simulations in Ansys Lumerical to characterize diffraction orders and electric field behavior — iterating on geometry parameters to optimize device performance.

  • Cleanroom Fabrication

    Trained in photolithography, spin coating, and e-beam metal deposition for semiconductor fabrication.

  • Recognition

    Best Project Award — California-Pacific-Northwest AI Hardware Hub & CITRIS showcase, 2025.
